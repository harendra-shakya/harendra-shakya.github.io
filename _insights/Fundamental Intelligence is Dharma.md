There is fundamental intelligence in the universe-
Food you eat gets digested without you (ego).
Trillions of galaxies are running on their own.
Fundamental principles like 2+2=4.

That fundamental force or intelligence is called [[ऋत]] (Rita). 
When Rita comes into ephemeral human life (व्याकवारिक जीवन) it is known as Dharma.

[[सनातन धर्म\|Dharma]] means "right way of living" or "natural way of living".

[[अहंकार\|Ego]] says I'm, I need to control everything. Our Dharma is to be egoless and live with natural fundamental intelligence.
