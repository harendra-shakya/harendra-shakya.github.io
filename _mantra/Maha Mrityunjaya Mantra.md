> ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम्।  
> उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात्॥

This mantra doesn't pray for protection from physical death, but **liberation from ego's trap**.

### Word-by-Word Meaning:

**Tryambakam Yajamahe**  
We worship the three-eyed Shiva.  
His third eye burns ignorance and duality.

**Sugandhim Pushtivardhanam**  
Fragrant, life-nourishing Shiva.  
Strengthens our true Self.

**Urvarukamiva Bandhanan**  
Like a ripe cucumber naturally detaches from the vine.  
Spiritual maturity breaks all bonds.

**Mrityor Mukshiya Maamritat**  
Free us from death's cycle.  
Not from fleeting world, but establish in eternal Truth.

### Core Message:

Don't just chant. Live with understanding. Let ego die, live in Truth.

**Transliteration**

> Om Tryambakam Yajamahe Sugandhim Pushtivardhanam  
> Urvarukamiva Bandhanan Mrityor Mukshiya Maamritat

This is the standard 24-syllable Vedic mantra from the Rigveda, chanted for protection and healing. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahamrityunjaya_Mantra)
