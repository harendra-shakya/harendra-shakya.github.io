**Definition:**  
A distinction between action driven by force and action arising from understanding.

|Discipline (Force)|Clarity (Understanding)|
|---|---|
|Effort-heavy|Effort-light|
|Motivated by reward/fear|Motivated by seeing truth|
|Temporary|Sustainable|
|Ego-driven|Intelligence-driven|

Insight:

Discipline without clarity leads to burnout.  
Clarity naturally expresses as order.
