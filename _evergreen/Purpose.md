Purpose, in the psychological sense, is the ego’s attempt to stabilize itself through **future-oriented object acquisition**.

**Structure:**  
Restlessness → Desire → Projection of Purpose → Pursuit → Temporary Meaning → Collapse

**Key Clarifications:**

- Purpose is usually not discovered; it is constructed.
- It replaces one object with another.
- It does not end restlessness—it sustains it.

**Core Insight:**  
**Purpose is often refined distraction.**
