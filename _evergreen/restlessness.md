Restlessness is the **felt sense of incompleteness** arising from misidentification.

**Structure:**  
[[Identification]] → [[Incompleteness]] → Movement → Seeking → Temporary Relief → Restlessness

**Key Clarifications:**

- It is not caused by lack of objects.
- It persists even after achievement.
- It is normalized socially, which hides its abnormality.

**Core Insight:**  
**Restlessness is not a problem to solve—it is a signal to investigate.**
