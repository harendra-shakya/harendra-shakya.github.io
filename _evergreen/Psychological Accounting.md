Inner accounting is the ego’s tendency to **record and evaluate action**:

- What was done
- What was gained
- What remains

This creates psychological burden:

- Expectation
- Regret
- Pride
- Fear

When action is recorded, it becomes transactional.

Freedom begins when this accounting ends.
