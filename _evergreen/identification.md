**Definition:**  
Identification is the act of **confusing what is perceived with the perceiver**.

**Structure:**  
Object → Psychological Attachment → Internalization → Identity

**Examples:**

- “My career”
- “My beliefs”
- “My suffering”

**Key Clarifications:**

- Identification is subtle and continuous.
- It converts neutral objects into psychological burdens.

**Core Insight:**  
**Where there is “mine,” there is bondage.**
