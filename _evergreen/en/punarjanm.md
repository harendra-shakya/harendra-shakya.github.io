**Core Idea**  
Every experience changes both observer and observed — each moment is a rebirth.

**Explanation**  
Every interaction transforms both sides.  
You are never exactly the same person again.

**Key Points**

- Experience = change
- Interaction is two-way transformation
- Even observation causes change
- Stable identity is an illusion

**One-Line Essence**  
A new “self” is born with every experience.
