> Kabir says: this world is blind — like a blind cow.  
> Its calf has already died, yet it keeps licking the empty hide.

> Kabir says: play your own drum for these few passing days.  
> This city, these streets — you shall not return to see them again. ~ [Sant Kabir](https://acharyaprashant.org/en/articles/sansar-beetata-dekhkar-bhee-sansar-samajh-naheen-aataa-sant-kabir-par-1_98bc089)

**Definition**
The world is the perception of a deluded “I.”

**Characteristics**

- Things appear inverted and distorted
- Imagination gets projected onto reality
- The experience feels true and convincing

**Core Principle**
Deluded self → Deluded world
