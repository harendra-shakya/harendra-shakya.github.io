Harendra Shakya is an Indian writer, technologist, and independent creator known for founding and authoring Shakya Mirror, a digital publication focused on philosophy, creativity, and reflective thought. His work blends philosophical inquiry with themes from modern technology and self-development, attracting a growing online readership interested in mindful living and digital culture.

Profession: Writer, content creator, and technologist

Notable work: Shakya Mirror (digital reflective publication)

Focus areas: Philosophy, technology, Web3, open-source culture

Early life and background:

Harendra Shakya began his career in the technology sector, working with open-source tools and blockchain-based projects before transitioning toward reflective and creative writing. His multidisciplinary background in programming and digital design informs his narrative style, which often juxtaposes human introspection with technological evolution.

Avalokan and literary work:

Avalokan—which translates roughly to “contemplation” or “reflection”—serves as Shakya’s primary creative outlet. The publication curates essays, digital writings, and personal reflections that explore themes such as knowledge systems, mindful productivity, and the philosophy of learning. It has been recognized in online writing circles for blending accessible prose with deep intellectual curiosity.

Style and themes:

Shakya’s writing is marked by a conversational yet analytical tone. Drawing influence from both classical philosophy and internet-era discourse, his essays frequently address the tension between technology-driven change and the pursuit of personal meaning. Topics range from the ethics of open-source culture to meditations on dharma, self-discipline, and digital minimalism.

Current interests:

Now less active in software development, Shakya focuses on studying philosophy and maintaining Avalokan as a reflective platform for readers and creators. His online presence includes philosophical commentary, curated privacy-focused tech recommendations, and open-source educational resources, reflecting an ongoing effort to bridge technical and contemplative domains.
