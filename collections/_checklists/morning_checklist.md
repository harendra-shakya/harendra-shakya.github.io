**Wake with Purpose**

- Set an intention for the day (e.g., "I create excellence in everything I do today").
- Take 3 deep breaths and ground yourself in the present moment.

**Express Gratitude**

- Write down 3 things you're grateful for.
- Reflect on why you’re grateful for them—deep, meaningful reflection.

**Mastery in Motion**

- **Yoga/Movement Practice**: Start your day with a practice (even 10-20 minutes) that embodies discipline and mindfulness.
- Focus on intentional movement, not just physical fitness. It’s about mastering your body to unlock mental clarity.

**Nourish Your Mind**

- Read something inspirational (philosophy, mythology, or wisdom).
- Take notes or jot down any insights that spark creativity or contemplation.

**Create First Thing**

- Spend 20-30 minutes working on something that excites you.
- **Create from love**: Whether it's a small project, a piece of writing, or brainstorming ideas, get into the habit of starting your day with creation.

**Set Your "If-Then" Conditions**

- Reflect on potential challenges you might face today and how you’ll handle them.
- Example: _If I get distracted, then I’ll take 3 deep breaths and refocus_.

**Inspiration Fuel**

- Collect something that inspires you today: an image, a quote, a moment of beauty.
- Let it fuel your actions and thoughts throughout the day.
