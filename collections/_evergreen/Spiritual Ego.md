**Definition:**  
Spiritual ego is the **[[identification]] with clarity, awareness, or progress itself**.

**Structure:**  
Insight → Ownership → Identity → Superiority/Subtle Separation

**Examples:**

- “I am more aware.”
- “I understand deeper truths.”
- “Others are unconscious.”

**Key Clarifications:**

- It is harder to detect than ordinary ego.
- It uses truth as a new identity.
- It blocks further inquiry.

**Core Insight:**  
**The ego survives by becoming subtle.**
