**Definition:**  
The difference between learning through direct testing and remaining stuck in pre-held beliefs.

Assumption → No Testing → Stagnation  
  
Experiment → Observation → Correction → Growth

Ego prefers assumption because:

- It avoids uncertainty
- It protects identity

Truth requires:

- Risk
- Openness
- Willingness to be wrong
